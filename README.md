## 👋 Who we are

We are the citizen's first call after something goes wrong online — a calm, honest team that guides you step by step and keeps you updated live.

We help people and small businesses with **hacked or suspended accounts, online financial fraud, frozen bank accounts and identity misuse** — and we build our own technology to stop fraud before it happens.

- 🧑‍💻 A real person reads every case
- 📍 Live status on every complaint
- 🗣️ English · हिन्दी · ગુજરાતી
- 🔒 We **never** ask for OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV, net-banking password or screen sharing

## 🔱 e-Trishul — Android safety app

> *"Stops fraud apps the moment they download."*

**28 security features · 4 protection layers · a real expert inside the app** · No ads · No data selling

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🛑 **Before install** | Install Shield, instant warning popup, Strict Mode, quarantine for fraud APKs |
| 📱 **On your phone** | Device scan, App Verifier, permissions manager, risky-file scan, Security Advisor |
| 🌐 **Online** | Website check, QR scan, Wi-Fi safety, police-ready report |
| 🆘 **After a fraud** | Report fraud, lost-money guide, stop call forwarding, chat with an expert |

<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.etrishul.digitalrakshak">📲 Get it on Google Play</a>
